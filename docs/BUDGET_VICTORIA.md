**Victoria-specific items**

- **Electrical permit** — City of Victoria contractor fee: $441 + 1.25 % of work value over $20,000, with owner-supplied equipment included in the value.
- **Building permit** — needed for the ground/carport-mounted share of the array: $100 application + 1.40 % of construction value. Roof mounts that meet the City's load/attachment/height conditions don't need one, but they do need a structural letter and module-level rapid shutdown (CEC Rule 64-218).
- **PST** — BC PST Bulletin 203 exempts PV panels, and the wiring, controllers and DC→AC devices bought as part of a PV system. **Batteries are explicitly not exempt**, and neither are general electrical items (panelboards, disconnect, splitter). The GridBOSS is treated as an exempt PV-system controller; confirm that with the supplier.
- **Incentives** — none included. BC Hydro's solar and battery rebates require a grid-connected, net-metered system; this design exports nothing.
- **Freight** — Vancouver Island delivery at 3 % of equipment value.

Forecast-aware dispatch saves **$1,052/yr (22 %)** on this design. It only charges the battery enough to reach the next forecast solar surplus, so generator energy doesn't displace sun that would otherwise be curtailed.
